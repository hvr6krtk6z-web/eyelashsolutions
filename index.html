Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

How long do lash extensions last?

With proper care, a premium set typically lasts around 3 to 6 weeks depending on your natural lash cycle and aftercare routine.

Can I wear makeup after treatment?

Yes, but it is best to keep things oil-free and avoid rubbing the eye area. A gentle oil-free remover is the safest option.

Do you offer mobile appointments?

Yes. Mobile treatments are available across London for clients who prefer appointments at home or at work.

What is included in a full set?

Every full set includes consultation, lash prep, precise individual application and guidance on aftercare and infill timing.
