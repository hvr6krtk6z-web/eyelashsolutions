FAQ

Helpful guidance before your appointment.

How long do lash extensions last?

Typically 3 to 6 weeks depending on your natural lash cycle, aftercare and how regularly you book infills.

Can I wear makeup after treatment?

Yes, but we recommend avoiding oily products and rubbing the eye area. Oil-free makeup remover is best.

Do you offer mobile appointments?

Yes. We provide mobile services across London for clients who prefer treatment at home or work.

When should I book a refill?

Most clients book every 3 to 4 weeks. This keeps the set looking full, neat and balanced.
